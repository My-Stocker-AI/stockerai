# Stocker AI - Quick Start Guide

**Voice-Guided Warehouse Stocking for Vending Machine Routes**

---

## Before You Start

### Browser Requirement

| Device | Recommended Browser |
|--------|---------------------|
| Windows Laptop | Chrome |
| Mac Laptop | Chrome (Safari has limitations) |
| Android Phone | Chrome |
| iPhone | Chrome or Safari (coming soon with enhanced support) |

**Why Chrome?** Stocker AI uses voice recognition that works best in Chrome. Safari on Mac can work but may require additional setup.

---

## Step 1: Create Your Account

1. Go to **[my-stocker-ai.com](https://my-stocker-ai.com)**

2. Click **"Sign Up"** tab

3. Fill in your details:
   - **First Name** - This is how the AI will greet you
   - **Last Name**
   - **Email** - Use a real email you can access
   - **Password** - Minimum 6 characters

4. Click **"Sign Up"**

5. You're in! No email confirmation needed.

---

## Step 2: Upload Your Route

Before you can start stocking, you need to upload your route data.

### Preparing Your Route PDF

Stocker AI works with **Parlevel route sheets** that include:
- Machine names/numbers
- Location names
- Product lists with slot numbers
- Pick quantities

### Uploading

1. Click the **"Upload"** tab at the bottom of the screen

2. Select the **delivery date** for this route

3. Click **"Choose File"** and select your route PDF

4. Click **"Upload Route"**

5. Wait for confirmation: *"Route uploaded successfully! [Route Name] for [Date], X machines, Y items"*

**Tip:** Upload your route the night before or morning of your delivery day.

---

## Step 3: Start Stocking

### Allow Microphone Access

The first time you use Stocker AI, your browser will ask for microphone permission.

1. Click **"Allow"** when prompted
2. If you accidentally clicked "Block", see the Troubleshooting section below

### Starting Your Route

1. Click the **"Voice"** tab at the bottom

2. The AI will greet you and ask about your routes

3. Say something like:
   - *"Start the South route for today"*
   - *"Begin my route for December 28th"*
   - *"What routes do I have?"*

4. The AI will confirm and give you your first item to pick

### During Stocking

The screen shows:
- **Top:** Current item to pick (large, easy to read)
- **Middle:** Voice status indicator
- **Bottom:** Completed items list

## Voice Commands

### Moving Through Items

| Say This | What Happens |
|----------|--------------|
| *"Done"* or *"Next"* | Marks current item complete, moves to next |
| *"Got it"* | Same as "Done" |
| *"Yes"* | Confirms the pick |

### Skipping & Going Back

| Say This | What Happens |
|----------|--------------|
| *"Skip this machine"* | Skips current machine, moves to next |
| *"Go back"* or *"Undo"* | Returns to previous item |
| *"Oops"* or *"Wait"* | Same as "Go back" |
| *"Go back to skipped"* | Returns to a machine you skipped earlier |

### Getting Information

| Say This | What Happens |
|----------|--------------|
| *"What's next?"* | Repeats current item |
| *"Where am I?"* | Tells you current machine and progress |
| *"How many left?"* | Tells you remaining items/machines |
| *"Repeat that"* | Repeats the last instruction |

### Pausing & Resuming

| Say This | What Happens |
|----------|--------------|
| *"Pause"* | Stops listening (take a break, phone call) |
| *"OK Stocker"* | Resumes after pause |
| *"Stop listening"* | Same as "Pause" |

**Tip:** You can also tap the Pause button on screen.

## Tips for Best Results

### Speaking Clearly

- Speak at normal volume and pace
- Wait for the AI to finish speaking before responding
- Short phrases work best: *"Done"*, *"Next"*, *"Skip machine"*

### Noisy Environments

- The AI handles warehouse noise well
- If it mishears you, just repeat your command
- Position your phone closer if needed

### Hands-Free Operation

- Clip your phone to your cart or pocket
- Use a Bluetooth earpiece for clearer audio
- The screen shows your current item even at a glance

## Session Resume

If you close the app or your browser accidentally:

1. Reopen **my-stocker-ai.com**
2. Log in with your account
3. You'll see: *"Resume Session?"*
4. Click **"Resume"** to continue where you left off
5. Or click **"Start Fresh"** to begin a new session

**Your progress is saved for 24 hours.**

## Troubleshooting

### "Microphone access denied"

**Chrome:**
1. Click the lock icon in the address bar
2. Find "Microphone" and change to "Allow"
3. Refresh the page

**Safari (Mac):**
1. Go to Safari menu → Settings → Websites → Microphone
2. Find my-stocker-ai.com and set to "Allow"
3. Also check: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → Safari

### Voice not responding

1. Check that your microphone is working (test in another app)
2. Make sure you're connected to the internet
3. Try refreshing the page
4. If using Safari, switch to Chrome

### AI says "No routes found"

1. Make sure you uploaded a route for today's date
2. Check the Upload tab to verify your route is there
3. Try saying the exact route name and date

### Audio not playing

1. Check your device volume
2. Make sure your phone isn't on silent mode
3. Try tapping the screen once (some browsers require interaction first)

## Safari on Mac - Additional Setup

If you must use Safari instead of Chrome:

### Enable Speech Recognition

1. Open **System Settings** (or System Preferences on older macOS)
2. Go to **Privacy & Security** → **Speech Recognition**
3. Enable for Safari

### Enable Dictation

1. Open **System Settings**
2. Go to **Keyboard** → **Dictation**
3. Turn Dictation **On**
4. Choose "Enhanced Dictation" if available

### Grant Microphone Access

1. Open **System Settings**
2. Go to **Privacy & Security** → **Microphone**
3. Enable for Safari

**Note:** Even with these settings, Safari may occasionally stop listening. Chrome provides a more reliable experience.
