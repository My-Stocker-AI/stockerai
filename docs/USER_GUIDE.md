# Stocker AI - User Guide
**Your Voice-Powered Stocking Assistant**

---

## What is Stocker?

Stocker is a voice assistant that reads your stocking list out loud, item by item, so you can pick products without looking at your phone. Just say "next" when you're ready, and Stocker tells you what to grab.

**The result:** Faster stocking, fewer mistakes, and your hands stay free the entire time.

---

## Getting Started (5 Minutes)

### Step 1: Install the App

1. On your phone, open **Safari** (iPhone) or **Chrome** (Android)
2. Go to **my-stocker-ai.com**
3. Tap the **Share** button (iPhone) or **Menu** (Android)
4. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
5. Now you have Stocker on your home screen like a regular app

### Step 2: Create Your Account

1. Open Stocker from your home screen
2. Tap **"Sign Up"**
3. Enter your email, first name, last name, and create a password
4. Tap **"Sign Up"** - you're in!

### Step 3: Upload Your First Route

1. Tap the **Upload** tab at the bottom
2. Tap **"Tap to select PDF"** and choose your Parlevel route PDF
3. Pick the **delivery date** (defaults to tomorrow)
4. Tap **"Upload Route"**
5. You'll see: "Route uploaded successfully! [Route Name] - X machines, Y items"

**That's it - you're ready to stock!**

## How to Stock (The Basics)

### Starting Your Session

1. Put in your **Bluetooth earbuds** (or use your phone speaker)
2. Open Stocker and tap **Voice** tab
3. Stocker greets you: *"Hey [Name]! You've got North Route for today. Ready to start?"*
4. Say **"Yes"** or the route name

### Picking Items

Stocker asks: *"Starting Machine 27 with 45 items. Top of the list or bottom?"*

Say **"top"** or **"bottom"** depending on how you want to work the machine.

Then Stocker reads:
> *"3 Doritos, slot 58"*

You grab 3 bags of Doritos, then say **"next"** (or "done", "got it", "okay" - whatever feels natural).

Stocker reads the next item. Repeat until the machine is done.

### When a Machine is Done

Stocker says: *"Done with Machine 27. Next is Machine 55. Top or bottom?"*

Just tell it your direction and keep going.

### When a Route is Done

Stocker says: *"Nice work! North Route is complete."*

If you have more routes, it offers: *"You also have South Route today. Want to start?"*

## Voice Commands (Quick Reference)

### Move to Next Item
Say any of these:
- **"Next"** / "next item"
- **"Done"** / "got it"
- **"Okay"** / "yep" / "yes"
- **"Check"** / "good" / "cool"

Or just **tap the item card** on screen.

### Take a Break
- **"Pause"** or **"wait"** or **"hold on"** - Stocker goes quiet
- Say **"OK Stocker"** to resume

### Skip a Machine
- **"Skip machine"** or **"skip this one"** - moves to next machine
- Stocker remembers skipped machines and asks about them later

### Go Back / Undo
- **"Undo"** or **"go back one"** - returns to previous item
- **"Oops"** or **"regroup"** - restart current machine from opposite direction

### Privacy Mode
- **"Mute"** - turns off microphone completely
- **"OK Stocker unmute"** or tap mic button to resume

## Tips for Best Results

### Audio Setup
- **Bluetooth earbuds work best** - clearer audio, hands completely free
- Phone speaker works too - just keep phone within a few feet
- Works great at warehouse volume levels

### Voice Tips
- **Speak naturally** - no need to yell or speak slowly
- **Short responses are fine** - "next" works as well as "okay next item please"
- **Warehouse noise is okay** - the system filters background sounds

### If Stocker Mishears You
- Just repeat yourself - it usually gets it the second time
- You can always **tap the screen** as a backup

### Battery & Connection
- Keep your phone **plugged in** for long sessions
- Works on WiFi or cellular - whichever is available
- If you lose connection, Stocker saves your place automatically

## Handling Common Situations

### "I grabbed the wrong amount"
Say **"undo"** - Stocker goes back to the previous item so you can fix it.

### "I need to skip this machine"
Say **"skip machine"** - Stocker moves to the next one and remembers to ask you about the skipped machine later.

### "I started from the wrong direction"
Say **"oops"** or **"regroup"** - Stocker asks "Top or bottom?" again so you can restart the machine correctly.

### "Someone's talking to me"
Say **"pause"** - Stocker goes quiet. When you're ready, say **"OK Stocker"** to continue.

### "My phone died"
No problem! When you restart and open Stocker, it asks if you want to continue where you left off. Your progress is saved after every item.

### "I need to check another date"
Say **"What routes do I have for Friday?"** (or any date) - Stocker looks it up.

## Uploading Routes

### Before Your Shift
1. Export your route PDFs from Parlevel
2. Open Stocker → Upload tab
3. Select each PDF and set the delivery date
4. Upload one route at a time (takes a few seconds each)

### What Gets Imported
- Route name
- All machines and their locations
- Every product with quantities
- Slot numbers for each item

### Supported Format
Currently works with **Parlevel Prekitting Detail** PDFs. The system automatically extracts all the data - no manual entry needed.

## Audio Beeps Explained

Stocker uses short beeps to give you feedback without words:

| Sound | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Two quick rising tones** | Item confirmed, moving to next |
| **Two falling tones** | Undo - going backward |
| **Single high beep** | Stocker finished talking, your turn |

This way you know what happened even in a noisy warehouse.

## Troubleshooting

### "Stocker isn't hearing me"
- Check that the green pulsing dot is showing (means it's listening)
- Make sure your mic isn't muted (no red icon)
- Try saying **"OK Stocker"** to wake it up

### "The voice sounds choppy"
- Check your internet connection
- Move closer to your WiFi router if possible
- Cellular usually works fine as backup

### "I can't hear Stocker"
- Check your phone volume
- Make sure Bluetooth is connected (if using earbuds)
- Try switching from earbuds to phone speaker

### "I connected my earbuds but can't hear or talk"
This happens when you connect Bluetooth earbuds AFTER opening Stocker. The browser keeps using the old audio device.

**Fix:**
1. Connect your earbuds first, THEN open Stocker
2. Or if already open: refresh the page (pull down on mobile, Cmd+Shift+R on Mac, Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows)
3. Allow microphone access again when prompted

**On Mac/laptop:** Also check System Preferences > Sound and make sure both Input AND Output are set to your earbuds.

### "My route isn't showing up"
- Make sure you uploaded it for the correct date
- Try saying the route name exactly as it appears
- Check the Upload tab to confirm it uploaded successfully

### "Upload failed - duplicate route"
This happens when you try to upload the same route for the same date twice. Stocker checks the **route name inside the PDF** (not the filename) combined with the delivery date.

- **To re-upload a corrected route:** First delete the existing route, then upload again
- **Renaming the PDF file won't help** - Stocker looks at the content, not the filename
- **Same route for a different date works fine** - each date is tracked separately
